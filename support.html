Common questions

Is halal screening a religious ruling (fatwa)?
No. Naseeha's screening is AI-assisted guidance based on AAOIFI standards, shown with an honest confidence level (Certified, Screened, Disagreement, or Insufficient Information). It's a starting point for your own research, not a substitute for consulting a qualified Shariah scholar.

Is Naseeha a licensed financial advisor?
No. Naseeha provides educational information only. Always consult a qualified financial or Shariah advisor before making investment decisions.

How do I delete my account and data?
Use "Clear All Data" in Settings, or email us at the address above and we'll handle it directly. See our Privacy Policy for full details on your data rights.
